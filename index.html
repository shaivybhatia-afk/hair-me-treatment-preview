Possible side effects

Some extra shedding at first

Common in the first few weeks, as older hairs make way for new growth. It is temporary.

Scalp irritation

Some people notice irritation on the scalp where they apply it. Tell your clinician if it happens.

Unwanted hair growth elsewhere

Hair may grow in places other than your scalp.
